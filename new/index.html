About

Hi, I am Ali ADALI. I am a Turkish Mathematician currently based in Toronto, ON

Welcome to my website. Here you can review my portfolio, explore my academic and creative work, learn about ongoing projects, evaluate opportunities for professional engagements, and connect via my social channels.

I offer expert instruction, consulting, and specialized services for both individuals and institutions across:

  • Math Olympiad instruction and contest coaching
  • Academic mathematics instruction (undergraduate and graduate levels)
  • Mathematical research and modeling
  • Educational content creation and custom interactive projects.

Please visit the Teaching & Coaching section for details on available courses and competition curricula. Please explore the Professional Engagements section to review service scopes or schedule an online consultation.
