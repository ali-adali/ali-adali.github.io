What is a Noipmahc?

While the Champion is the final node of the winning sequence, the Noipmahc is the starting player of the longest losing chain: they lose in Round 1, their opponent loses in Round 2, and so on, propagating directly to the tournament winner.

Graph Theory & Algorithmic Proofs

1. Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG) Formulation:

Let $G = (V, E)$ represent the tournament, where a directed edge $a \to b$ signifies that player $a$ beat player $b$. The Champion is the root node of maximum in-degree across round progressions.

2. Edge-Reversal Symmetry:

Construct $G^{op} = (V, E^{op})$ by reversing all directed edges ($b \to a$ if $a$ beat $b$). Finding the Champion in $G^{op}$ yields the Noipmahc of $G$.

3. Linear Time Complexity:

Since knockout draws form single-elimination trees, traversing backwards from the Champion node to find the Noipmahc takes $\mathcal{O}(R)$ time, where $R = \log_2(N)$ rounds.
