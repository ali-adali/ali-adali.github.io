What is a Noipmahc? Noipmahc (Champion spelled backwards) is the anti-champion. ChampioN - ultimate winner of the longest winning chain ⇄ NoipmahC - ultimate loser of the longest losing chain
the Algoritm the Noipmahc is found by tracing the chain of defeat backward. Let C1 be the champion. - Let C2 be who lost to C1 in the final. - Let C3 be who lost to C2 in the previous round. In general Ck+1 is lost to Ck in the next round. - Continue this path (C4 ,...,Cn ) until it stops at Cn . Cn is the noipmahc.
🏆 🔄 🏆 If every match result in a single-elimination bracket (turning all wins into losses) is reversed, the original Champion becomes the Noipmahc, and the original Noipmahc becomes the new Champion!
